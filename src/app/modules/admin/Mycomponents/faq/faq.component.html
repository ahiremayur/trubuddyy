FAQs


How does Trubuddyy work?

At Trubuddyy, you have the opportunity to converse with experienced professionals who have traversed the path that you aspire to take. These knowledgeable individuals are referred to as 'Mentors'.
Trubuddyy offers the opportunity to engage in a one-on-one conversation with a Mentor of your choice. You can simply log in to the website, browse through the available Mentors, and schedule a video conversation with the one that best fits your needs.

Who are the Mentor’s?

Mentors on Trubuddyy are experts in their respective fields, with diverse educational backgrounds and professional experiences. They are accomplished individuals who have come together to share their knowledge and insights with others. Trubuddyy is a community of mentors from various walks of life, ranging from chartered accountants to fashion designers to sports personalities.

Are Trubuddyy conversations paid?

Indeed, all one-on-one conversations on Trubuddyy require payment. We believe that it's essential to value a professional's time and compensate them monetarily for their expertise. We are of the opinion that paying a nominal fee is worthwhile since it can save your valuable time and provide you with accurate information from the right person so that you can have a long successful career.

How does a Mentor conversation help?

Speaking with a mentor can be highly beneficial in various ways. As mentors have already traversed the path that you aspire to follow, they can relate to your situation and comprehend your perspective. Therefore, conversations with mentors are not only free of judgment but also highly productive, allowing you to share ideas, receive feedback, gain unique insights, and engage in effective brainstorming sessions.

What can I talk about in a 1-on-1 Conversation?

Trubuddyy provides a platform where you can have a conversation about anything related to your career and education, including your studies, work experiences, and different courses, among other educational topics. However, it is important to note that the conversations should remain focused on career and education-related questions, and thus personal questions are not allowed.

What happens after I book a 1-on-1 Conversation?

After you have booked a conversation on Trubuddyy, we will send you a confirmation email. Additionally, you will receive an email with an invitation link that you are required to click on at the scheduled time of the conversation. Please note that neither party's contact information (including email or phone number) is shared before or after the conversation.

What happens in a 1-on-1 Conversation?

The 1-on-1 conversations on video are usually 45 minutes long and do not have a fixed structure. You are free to discuss any career or education related doubts that are on your mind which you need help on.
We encourage that conversations remain organic and take their natural course. No prior preparation is needed!

How do I select a Mentor?

To select the right Mentor on trubuddyy, it is recommended to find someone who has studied in the field you are interested in and has some similarities to you. You can use the search bar on the website to find Mentors with specific degrees and choose from the search results. It's important to take note of the Mentor's age, languages spoken, and relevant experiences before booking a conversation with them.

Trubuddyy provides a platform where you can have a conversation about anything related to your career and education, including your studies, work experiences, and different courses, among other educational topics. However, it is important to note that the conversations should remain focused on career and education-related questions, and thus personal questions are not allowed.

What happens if I am unable to make it for a Conversation?

While we understand that there may be circumstances that prevent you from attending a scheduled conversation, we do not offer refunds as we want to respect our mentors' time.

What happens after a 1-on-1 Conversation?

We value your feedback on Trubuddyy conversations and will send you a feedback form via email. Additionally, you are welcome to book another conversation slot with the same Mentor or a different Mentor for the same or different course.

Is what I discuss kept confidential?

All conversations, interactions, and observations with Mentors on Trubuddyy are kept confidential and private. During the conversations, both the Mentor and you may share personal experiences, and it is important to keep the content of the conversation limited to just the two of you. No contact information, such as email or phone number, is shared before or after the conversation to maintain privacy.

Can I have an in-person conversation?

Currently all Mentor conversations on Trubuddyy are done online via Zoom.

Can we chat 1-on-1 with a Mentor?

The option to chat with your mentor will only be available after you have booked a session with them. The chat feature will only be accessible 24 hours before the scheduled video call.

What if I don’t get a relevant person to talk to?

In case you cannot find a suitable Mentor on our platform, you can reach out to us by filling out the form on the "Contact Us" page or sending us a message on WhatsApp. We will do our best to find a relevant Mentor for you from our extended network and then offer you a free session.

How can I know if the Mentor experience is genuine?

Each Mentor on Trubuddyy fills out a comprehensive form that describes their professional journey, along with other essential details about themselves. Furthermore, we conduct a verification video call with them to comprehend their motivation for becoming a Mentor and to discuss their education and experience.

What if I don’t get my required slots?

In case you are unable to find a suitable Mentor for a specific time slot, you can reach out to us through the WhatsApp widget on the website. We will reach out to relevant Mentors and inquire if they are available for a conversation during the required time slots.
